## 0.1.0

- Run Lite Scan: the GEO scores of a website, free of charge.
- Run Full Scan: the first full results of a website, for 1 credit.
- Get Full Report: the full report of a scan by its ID.
- Connect with a button (OAuth2); disconnect in GEO Search Leads under Settings → Connections.
